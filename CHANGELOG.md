# Changelog

## [1.2.0] — 2026-10-08

First public release.

- Translates text, PDF and Word files on the Mac with TranslateGemma; nothing is sent anywhere.
- PDF and Word files keep their layout; Markdown, `.srt` and `.csv` keep their structure.
- Selected text in any app with ⌃⌥T, any area of the screen with ⌃⌥O.
- Listen with the Mac's voices or with Qwen3-TTS, a neural voice that runs on the Mac.
- The interface in 13 languages: English, Russian, Ukrainian, Spanish, French, German,
  Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Turkish, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
- Five appearances: Pink, Glass, Forest, Light and Dark, or the one macOS uses.
- Tone, style and a glossary; a list of things to check after each translation.
